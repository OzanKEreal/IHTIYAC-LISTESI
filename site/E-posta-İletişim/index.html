    E-POSTA VE İLETİŞİM¶

    Email Hosting ve Services¶

    1. Gmail (Google Workspace)¶

    • Platform: Web, iOS, Android, Desktop clients
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (@gmail.com) / Ücretli ($6-18/month for Workspace)
    • Özellikers:
    • 15GB storage (free) / 30GB+ (paid)
    • Spam filtering
    • Labels
    • Priority Inbox
    • Smart Compose
    • Integration (Google ecosystem)
    • Multiple accounts
    • Vacation responders
    • Search
    • En İyi Kullanım: Personal ve business email
    • Web: https://mail.google.com

    2. Outlook (Microsoft 365)¶

    • Platform: Web, Windows, macOS, iOS, Android
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (@outlook.com) / Ücretli ($6-12.50/month for Microsoft 365)
    • Özellikers:
    • Microsoft ecosystem
    • Focused Inbox
    • Calendar integration
    • Tasks (To Do)
    • Contacts
    • File attachment (OneDrive)
    • Rules
    • Folders
    • Search
    • En İyi Kullanım: Microsoft 365 email
    • Web: https://outlook.com

    3. ProtonMail¶

    • Platform: Web, iOS, Android
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (limited) / Ücretli ($4-20/month)
    • Özellikers:
    • End-to-end encryption
    • Swiss-based
    • Self-destructing emails
    • Custom domains (paid)
    • Multiple aliases
    • Calendar (paid)
    • VPN included (paid)
    • No tracking
    • En İyi Kullanım: Privacy-focused email
    • Web: https://protonmail.com

    4. Zoho Mail¶

    • Platform: Web, iOS, Android
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (1 user) / Ücretli ($1-6/month)
    • Özellikers:
    • Custom domains
    • Spam protection
    • Calendar
    • Tasks
    • Contacts
    • Zoho ecosystem integration
    • Multiple aliases
    • Email encryption
    • Migration tools
    • En İyi Kullanım: Business email
    • Web: https://www.zoho.com/mail

    5. Fastmail¶

    • Platform: Web, iOS, Android, Desktop clients
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz deneme / Ücretli ($3-9/month)
    • Özellikers:
    • Fast email
    • Custom domains
    • Encryption
    • Multiple aliases
    • Snooze
    • Reminders
    • Calendar
    • Contacts
    • No tracking
    • En İyi Kullanım: Fast reliable email
    • Web: https://www.fastmail.com

    Email Client'ları¶

    6. Mozilla Thunderbird¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz
    • Özellikers:
    • Open source
    • IMAP/POP3/SMTP
    • Customizable
    • Extensions
    • Filters
    • Address book
    • Calendar
    • PGP encryption
    • Multiple accounts
    • En İyi Kullanım: Free open-source email client
    • Web: https://www.thunderbird.net

    7. Apple Mail¶

    • Platform: macOS, iOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (Apple ecosystem)
    • Özellikers:
    • Built-in Apple
    • IMAP/POP3/SMTP
    • iCloud integration
    • Smart mailboxes
    • VIP senders
    • Exchange support
    • PGP encryption
    • Spam filter
    • En İyi Kullanım: Mac and iOS email
    • Web: https://www.apple.com/macos/mail/

    8. Windows Mail¶

    • Platform: Windows
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (Windows built-in)
    • Özellikers:
    • Built-in Windows
    • IMAP/POP3/SMTP
    • Sync with Outlook.com
    • Gmail support
    • Exchange support
    • Calendar integration
    • Simple interface
    • En İyi Kullanım: Windows default email
    • Web: Built-in to Windows

    9. Microsoft Outlook (Desktop)¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Microsoft 365)
    • Özellikers:
    • Desktop email client
    • IMAP/POP3/SMTP
    • Exchange support
    • Calendar integration
    • Tasks
    • Contacts
    • Rules
    • Categories
    • Focused Inbox
    • En İyi Kullanım: Microsoft 365 desktop email
    • Web: https://www.microsoft.com/outlook

    10. Spark¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, iOS, Android
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz / Ücretli (Teams - $7.99/user/month)
    • Özellikers:
    • Smart inbox
    • Smart notifications
    • Email delegation
    • Snooze
    • Follow-up reminders
    • Email templates
    • Collaboration (Teams)
    • Multiple accounts
    • En İyi Kullanım: Smart email client
    • Web: https://sparkmailapp.com
