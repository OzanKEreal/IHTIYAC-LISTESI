    FOTOĞRAF DÜZENLEME VE İŞLEME¶

    Profesyonel Fotoğraf Editörleri¶

    1. Adobe Photoshop¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Adobe Creative Cloud - $20.99/ay)
    • Özellikler:
    • Industry standard
    • Layer-based editing
    • AI-powered features (Neural Filters)
    • Generative Fill
    • Content-Aware Fill
    • Camera RAW
    • Advanced masking
    • 3D editing
    • Plugins support
    • En İyi Kullanım: Profesyonel fotoğraf manipülasyonu
    • Web: https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop.html

    2. Adobe Lightroom¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Web
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($9.99-19.99/ay)
    • Özellikler:
    • Non-destructive editing
    • RAW processing
    • Preset library
    • Organize & share
    • Cloud sync
    • AI masking
    • Batch editing
    • HDR merge
    • Panorama stitch
    • En İyi Kullanım: Fotoğraf yönetimi ve RAW düzenleme
    • Web: https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html

    3. Capture One¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($29/yıl veya $29-299 one-time)
    • Özellikler:
    • RAW processing
    • Tethered shooting
    • Color grading
    • Advanced editing
    • Sessions & catalogs
    • Layer editing (Pro)
    • Film grain
    • Lens correction
    • En İyi Kullanım: Profesyonel RAW processing ve color grading
    • Web: https://www.captureone.com

    4. DxO PhotoLab¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($139-219 one-time)
    • Özellikler:
    • AI-powered denoising (DeepPRIME)
    • Optical corrections
    • Local adjustments
    • RAW processing
    • Color control
    • Lens sharpness
    • Exposure control
    • U-Point technology
    • En İyi Kullanım: RAW optimization ve lens corrections
    • Web: https://www.dxo.com/dxo-photolab

    5. Luminar Neo¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($3.16/ay veya $59-119 one-time)
    • Özellikler:
    • AI-powered editing
    • Sky Replacement
    • Portrait enhancement
    • HDR Merge
    • Supersharp AI
    • Noiseless AI
    • Relight AI
    • Layers & masking
    • En İyi Kullanım: AI tabanlı hızlı fotoğraf düzenleme
    • Web: https://skylum.com/luminar
