    Social Media Management Platforms¶

    1. Hootsuite¶

    • Platform: Web, iOS, Android
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz deneme / Ücretli ($99-739/month)
    • Özellikers:
    • Multi-platform scheduling
    • Analytics
    • Content curation
    • Team collaboration
    • Approvals
    • Bulk scheduling
    • Social listening
    • Advertising management
    • En İyi Kullanım: Comprehensive social media management
    • Web: https://hootsuite.com
