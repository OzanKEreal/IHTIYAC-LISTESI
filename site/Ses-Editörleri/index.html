    SES EDİTÖRLERİ VE MÜZİK PRODÜKSİYONU¶

    Profesyonel DAW (Digital Audio Workstation)¶

    1. Ableton Live¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Intro $99, Standard $449, Suite $799)
    • Özellikler:
    • Session ve Arrangement views
    • Max for Live
    • Advanced warping
    • MIDI kontrol
    • Audio effects
    • Synth presetleri
    • Live looping
    • Performans odaklı
    • En İyi Kullanım: Canlı performans ve elektronik müzik prodüksiyonu
    • Web: https://www.ableton.com

    2. FL Studio¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Fruity $99, Producer $199, Signature $299, All Plugins Bundle $899)
    • Özellikler:
    • Pattern-based sequencing
    • Piano roll
    • Step sequencer
    • VST/VSTi desteği
    • Lifetime free updates
    • Mixer
    • Automation
    • Audio recording
    • En İyi Kullanım: Hip-hop, trap ve elektronik müzik
    • Web: https://www.image-line.com/flstudio/

    3. Avid Pro Tools¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Perpetual $599, Subscriptions $29.99/ay)
    • Özellikler:
    • Industry standard
    • Advanced automation
    • Cloud collaboration
    • HD audio
    • Mixing console
    • Post-production
    • Video integration
    • Dolby Atmos support
    • En İyi Kullanım: Profesyonel stüdyo ve post-production
    • Web: https://www.avid.com/pro-tools

    4. Logic Pro¶

    • Platform: macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($199.99)
    • Özellikler:
    • Mac optimize
    • Apple Loops
    • Flex Time/Pitch
    • Drummer
    • Music notation
    • Remote mixing
    • Spatial Audio
    • Alchemy synthesizer
    • En İyi Kullanım: Mac kullanıcıları için profesyonel prodüksiyon
    • Web: https://www.apple.com/logic-pro/

    5. Steinberg Cubase¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Elements $99, Artist $299, Pro $579)
    • Özellikler:
    • MIDI sequencing
    • Audio recording
    • Mixing console
    • Sample Editor
    • VST3 desteği
    • Score Editor
    • Chord Track
    • Sampler Track
    • En İyi Kullanım: Genel müzik prodüksiyonu ve MIDI çalışması
    • Web: https://www.steinberg.net/cubase

    Ücretsiz ve Açık Kaynak DAW¶

    6. Audacity¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz ve Açık Kaynak
    • Özellikler:
    • Multi-track recording
    • Audio editing
    • Effect plugins
    • Noise reduction
    • VST support
    • Macros
    • Spectogram view
    • Export çeşitliliği
    • En İyi Kullanım: Basit ses düzenleme ve podcast prodüksiyonu
    • Web: https://www.audacityteam.org

    7. GarageBand¶

    • Platform: macOS, iOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz
    • Özellikler:
    • Apple ekosistemi
    • Loops library
    • Virtual instruments
    • Drummer
    • Guitar amps
    • Voice recording
    • Easy learning
    • Logic Pro upgrade
    • En İyi Kullanım: Yeni başlayanlar ve Apple kullanıcıları
    • Web: https://www.apple.com/mac/garageband/

    8. LMMS¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz ve Açık Kaynak
    • Özellikler:
    • Song editor
    • Beat+Bassline editor
    • Piano roll
    • FX Mixer
    • VSTi support
    • Built-in instruments
    • Automation
    • MIDI support
    • En İyi Kullanım: Ücretsiz DAW alternatifi
    • Web: https://lmms.io

    9. Ardour¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (Source) / Ücretli (Binary - $45-120)
    • Özellikler:
    • Unlimited tracks
    • Plugin automation
    • Non-destructive editing
    • LADSPA, LV2, VST support
    • MIDI sequencing
    • Video timeline
    • Scripting
    • En İyi Kullanım: Profesyonel audio production
    • Web: https://ardour.org

    10. Cakewalk by BandLab¶

    • Platform: Windows
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz
    • Özellikler:
    • Professional DAW
    • Melodyne integration
    • ProChannel modules
    • VocalSync
    • Mix Recall
    • Matrix view
    • MIDI scripting
    • En İyi Kullanım: Windows için ücretsiz profesyonel DAW
    • Web: https://www.bandlab.com/products/cakewalk

    Mid-Range DAW¶

    11. PreSonus Studio One¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Artist $99.95, Professional $399.95, Professional+ $499.95)
    • Özellikler:
    • Drag-and-drop workflow
    • Scratch Pads
    • Show Page
    • Integrated mastering
    • Chord Track
    • Note Editor
    • Remote app
    • En İyi Kullanım: Esnek ve modern prodüksiyon
    • Web: https://www.presonus.com/products/Studio-One

    12. REAPER¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($60 discount, $225 full)
    • Özellikler:
    • Hafif ve hızlı
    • Unrestricted trial
    • Customizable
    • Plugin support
    • Scripting (EEL, Lua, Python)
    • Multi-language
    • 64-bit processing
    • En İyi Kullanım: Esnek ve özelleştirilebilir prodüksiyon
    • Web: https://www.reaper.fm

    13. Bitwig Studio¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($299-599)
    • Özellikler:
    • Modular design
    • Clip launcher
    • The Grid
    • Modulators
    • Hardware integration
    • Open file format
    • Cross-platform
    • En İyi Kullanım: Modern ve modular prodüksiyon
    • Web: https://www.bitwig.com

    14. MOTU Digital Performer¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($499)
    • Özellikler:
    • Film scoring
    • Music notation
    • Audio tracks
    • MIDI sequencing
    • Virtual instruments
    • Cloud collaboration
    • 64-bit mixing
    • En İyi Kullanım: Film scoring ve comprehensive production
    • Web: https://www.motu.com/products/software/dp

    Specialized Audio Tools¶

    15. Adobe Audition¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Adobe CC - $20.99/ay)
    • Özellikler:
    • Waveform editing
    • Multitrack session
    • Spectral display
    • Noise reduction
    • Essential Sound panel
    • Auto-ducking
    • Remix
    • En İyi Kullanım: Post-production ve podcasting
    • Web: https://www.adobe.com/products/audition.html

    16. Sound Forge¶

    • Platform: Windows
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Sound Forge Audio Cleaning Lab $49.99, Sound Forge Pro $399)
    • Özellikler:
    • Audio editing
    • Mastering
    • Recording
    • Restoration
    • VST effects
    • Spectral editing
    • 64-bit processing
    • En İyi Kullanım: Mastering ve audio editing
    • Web: https://www.magix.com/int/sound-forge/

    17. iZotope RX¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli ($129-1499)
    • Özellikler:
    • Audio repair
    • Spectral editing
    • De-noise, De-click
    • De-hum, De-reverb
    • Dialogue isolate
    • Music rebalance
    • Machine learning
    • En İyi Kullanım: Audio repair ve restoration
    • Web: https://www.izotope.com/en/products/rx.html

    18. OcenAudio¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz
    • Özellikler:
    • Real-time preview
    • Cross-platform
    • Multi-selection
    • Effect chain editor
    • VST plugins
    • Spectogram
    • File formats
    • En İyi Kullanım: Hızlı ve basit ses düzenleme
    • Web: https://www.ocenaudio.com

    19. TwistedWave¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS, iOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretsiz (iOS) / Ücretli (Desktop $119.90)
    • Özellikler:
    • Audio editing
    • Batch processing
    • Voice-over recording
    • Effects
    • Automatic silence
    • Direct access to cloud
    • En İyi Kullanım: Voice-over ve podcast editing
    • Web: https://twistedwave.com

    20. Hindenburg Journalist¶

    • Platform: Windows, macOS
    • Fiyat: Ücretli (Journalist Pro $399, Educator $495)
    • Özellikler:
    • Podcast odaklı
    • Auto leveling
    • One-click publishing
    • Voice profiling
    • Sound effects library
    • Chapter markers
    • En İyi Kullanım: Profesyonel podcast prodüksiyonu
    • Web: https://hindenburg.com
